## Project Overview
CampusNest is a robust Hostel Management System designed to streamline daily operations and management tasks:

- **Student & Room Management**: Tracking student records and housing allocations.
- **Mess Management**: Handling daily food menus and warden status tracking.
- **Hostel Shop Inventory**: Managing stock and availability for essential items.
- **Leave Management System**: Tracking student leaves with warden oversight.
- **Service Desk (RMS)**: A hostel living issue register for reporting and tracking maintenance concerns.
- **Warden/Admin-Assisted Workflows**: Designed for use by staff to ensure efficient hostel administration.
